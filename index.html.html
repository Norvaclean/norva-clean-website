Everything you need to know about booking with Nørva Clean.

Pricing is based on apartment size, service type, and frequency. We provide instant quotes via our booking form or WhatsApp. Most standard 2-bedroom cleans start from 250 PLN. Regular clients receive preferred pricing.

Yes. We bring all premium eco-friendly cleaning products and professional equipment. You don't need to provide anything. If you have preferred products for specific surfaces, we're happy to use them.

Typically 2–4 hours depending on apartment size, including linen change and restocking. We can often handle same-day bookings for existing clients. We coordinate directly with check-in/check-out times.
